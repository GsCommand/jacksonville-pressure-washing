Pool cage cleaning Jacksonville FL

A pool cage is a system of screens, coated aluminum, doors and elevated panels—not one surface

Jacksonville homeowners search for pool cage cleaning, pool enclosure cleaning, pool cage pressure washing and pool screen cleaning. The service is more complicated than those phrases make it sound. A pool enclosure can combine delicate screen mesh, painted or coated aluminum framing, roof-screen panels, hardware, door assemblies and a pool deck below. Each part has different limits.

Clean the enclosure without using concrete-cleaning pressure on the screens

The goal of pool-cage cleaning is to remove removable exterior buildup while respecting the screen and frame condition. High-pressure spot blasting can stretch or tear older mesh, disturb loose spline, drive water where it is not wanted, or make an oxidized frame look uneven.

A controlled cleaning process relies on inspection, appropriate treatment, lower rinse pressure and deliberate work around doors, roof panels and enclosure edges.

Pool cage pressure washing: homeowners use the phrase, but the best method is usually not maximum pressure. Screen age, frame coating, buildup and access should control the process.
Pool enclosure surfaces

What actually gets cleaned on a Jacksonville pool cage?

A useful quote should define which parts of the enclosure are included. “Pool cage cleaning” can mean very different things from one property to another.

Aluminum Frames & Crossmembers

Vertical posts, horizontal rails and roof framing collect pollen, algae, dust and cobwebs. Coating condition matters because chalking, faded paint and oxidation are not ordinary dirt.

Screen Mesh

Screen panels collect fine organic film, pollen, dust and insect debris. Mesh material, age, tension, tears and spline condition should be checked before cleaning because fragile screens need conservative handling.

Roof Screen Panels

Upper and roof panels often show the heaviest buildup because they are exposed to trees, rain and airborne debris. Their condition and access determine whether they can be cleaned safely from the available working position.

Doors, Thresholds & Ledges

Entry doors, lower rails, corners and ledges trap dirt, algae and insect debris. These details are easy to miss if a job is approached as a quick rinse instead of enclosure cleaning.

Cleaning is not repair: ripped mesh, loose spline, corroded fasteners, damaged doors, bent aluminum, failing coatings and structural issues should be repaired rather than hidden by a cleaning claim.
Pool cage soft washing

Soft washing, rinsing and pressure all have different jobs

Pool enclosures are a good example of why “pressure washing” should not mean one fixed pressure setting. The same enclosure may need treatment-focused cleaning on frames and screens while the concrete deck below can tolerate a completely different process.

Use the least aggressive method that handles the buildup

Organic film can often be loosened with an appropriate exterior-cleaning treatment so the rinse does not have to do all the work mechanically. Screens and coated aluminum benefit from avoiding unnecessary concentrated pressure.

That does not mean every dark mark is biological buildup. Frame oxidation, chalking, paint failure, rust at fasteners and staining from nearby irrigation or metal can require different expectations or specialty treatment.

Florida pool cage buildup

Why pool enclosures get dirty so quickly in Northeast Florida

Warm temperatures, humidity, shade and constant outdoor exposure make pool cages natural collection points for organic growth and airborne debris.

Green Organic Growth

Shaded lower rails, screens and frame joints can develop green buildup where surfaces stay damp longer after rain or irrigation.

Pollen & Tree Debris

Fine pollen and tree material collect on screen mesh and roof panels, especially on cages near mature landscaping.

Cobwebs & Insect Debris

Upper corners, doors and frame intersections trap cobwebs, small nests and insect debris that make an otherwise clean pool area look neglected.

Dark Film on Screens

Screen mesh can hold a combination of dust, organic film and airborne grime that reduces the clean, open appearance of the enclosure.

Frame Chalking

Weathered aluminum coatings can become chalky or faded. Cleaning may expose that condition more clearly but does not restore damaged coating.

Deck-to-Cage Splashback

Dirty pool decks and pavers can splash material onto lower screens and rails. Cleaning the cage and deck together can make sense when both are part of the problem.

Pool cage cleaning process

A six-step process for screens, frames and the pool environment

The exact method changes with enclosure condition, but the planning sequence should stay consistent.

1Inspect screens and frame conditionLook for torn or loose mesh, damaged spline, oxidation, failing paint, corrosion, loose doors and areas that should not be aggressively cleaned.
2Define the cleaning scopeConfirm whether the quote includes inside faces, outside faces, roof screens, doors, ledges, pool deck or only selected parts of the enclosure.
3Prepare the pool areaAccount for furniture, pool equipment, electrical fixtures, plants, drainage and the pool itself before applying cleaning treatment.
4Treat compatible buildupApply surface-appropriate treatment where useful so algae and organic film can be loosened without relying on excessive mechanical pressure.
5Rinse frames and screens carefullyUse controlled rinsing appropriate to screen condition and frame coating, working around doors, roof panels and enclosure edges.
6Review remaining conditionsSeparate removable buildup from oxidation, corrosion, permanent staining, damaged coatings or screen repairs that cleaning cannot correct.
Pool cage cleaning vs pool deck cleaning

The cage and the deck are related—but they are not the same cleaning service

One of the easiest ways to damage a pool area is to use the method intended for one surface on another. Screen mesh and aluminum framing should not be treated like concrete flatwork.

Separate the enclosure from the floor beneath it

A concrete pool deck may be cleaned with controlled flatwork equipment. Pavers, painted decks and coatings can require different methods. The pool cage above needs a lower-pressure, detail-oriented process because the screens, frame coating and elevated panels have different failure points.

If you want both areas cleaned, the quote can define each part of the project. See the dedicated Pool Deck Cleaning page for deck-specific guidance.

Best scope: cage only, deck only, or both should be stated clearly before the job so there is no assumption that “pool area cleaning” automatically includes every surface.
Pool cage cleaning near me

What to look for when hiring a pool enclosure cleaning company

A good pool cage cleaner should be able to explain how the method changes between screen mesh, aluminum framing and the deck below—not simply advertise the strongest pressure washer.

Screen Inspection

Older or damaged mesh should be identified before washing instead of discovering weak panels after aggressive pressure has already been applied.

Oxidation Awareness

The company should distinguish removable organic buildup from chalking, faded coating, corrosion and permanent discoloration.

Defined Roof-Screen Scope

If the upper roof panels are part of the job, access and screen condition should be discussed rather than assumed.

Pool & Plant Planning

The cleaning process should consider pool water, nearby vegetation, furniture, fixtures and drainage before treatment begins.

Separate Deck Method

Concrete, pavers or coated pool decks should be evaluated separately from the screen enclosure instead of receiving one universal process.

Realistic Results

No cleaning company should promise that washing will repair damaged screens, restore corroded aluminum or reverse failing paint.

Pool cage cleaning FAQ

Questions Jacksonville homeowners ask before cleaning a pool enclosure

What is included in pool cage cleaning?

Compatible aluminum framing, screen panels, roof-screen areas, enclosure doors, ledges and other enclosure surfaces can be included when they are part of the quote. Pool-deck cleaning is separate unless specifically included.

Can pool cage screens be pressure washed?

Screen mesh should not be treated like concrete. Screen age, tension, tears, spline and frame condition should determine the cleaning pressure and technique.

Does pool cage cleaning remove green algae and mildew?

Organic buildup on compatible frames and screens can often be treated and rinsed. Oxidation, failing paint, corrosion and permanent discoloration are different conditions.

Can you clean the roof screens on a pool enclosure?

Roof-screen areas can be included when access and condition allow. Loose, brittle or damaged roof panels may need repair instead of cleaning.

Will pool cage cleaning fix chalky or oxidized aluminum?

No. Standard cleaning removes surface buildup; it does not restore failed coating, oxidation or corrosion.

Is the pool deck included with pool cage cleaning?

Only if it is included in the quoted scope. Pool decks require their own surface-specific cleaning decisions.

How is the pool and landscaping protected during cleaning?

The plan should account for pool water, drainage, plants, furniture, electrical fixtures and adjacent surfaces. Protection and rinse procedures depend on the property and treatment.

How do I get a quote for pool cage cleaning in Jacksonville?

Send the property address and photos, and note whether you want inside/outside surfaces, roof screens, the pool deck or other nearby areas included.
