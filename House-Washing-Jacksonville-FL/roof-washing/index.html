Roof washing should treat growth, not blast shingles

Residential roof washing is generally approached with a soft-wash process rather than aggressive high pressure.

Roof type, age, visible damage, pitch, drainage, gutters and landscaping are reviewed before cleaning.

Typical process

  • Inspect roof material and access
  • Protect landscaping and fixtures
  • Apply low-pressure treatment
  • Review remaining streaks
