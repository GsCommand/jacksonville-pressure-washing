Clay County exterior cleaning

Concrete, shaded exteriors and outdoor living areas all need different cleaning plans

Clay County homes can combine concrete driveways, stucco or siding, shingle roofs, gutters, fences and screened outdoor spaces. Dark organic buildup, irrigation staining and ordinary weather exposure do not respond the same way on every material.

Driveway and concrete cleaning

Concrete driveways, sidewalks and curbs can usually tolerate more cleaning pressure than a house exterior, but even flatwork requires consistent technique and stain-specific treatment when oil, rust or irrigation staining is involved.

Driveway Cleaning covers the concrete process in more detail.

House, roof and fence cleaning

Sensitive surfaces should not be cleaned like flatwork

House Washing

Compatible stucco, siding, painted surfaces, soffits, fascia and trim are evaluated for lower-pressure cleaning.

Roof Cleaning

Shingle, tile and compatible roofing systems require roof-specific methods based on material and condition.

Fence Cleaning

Vinyl, PVC and other compatible fences are evaluated for organic buildup, oxidation and coating condition.

Gutter Cleaning

Interior debris removal and accessible downspout checks are separate from exterior gutter brightening.

Pool Areas

Pool decks, aluminum cage frames and screen mesh each require different cleaning decisions.

Specialty Stains

Rust, irrigation staining, oil and oxidation are evaluated separately from ordinary organic growth.
