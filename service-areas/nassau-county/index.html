Nassau County exterior cleaning

Growing communities, wooded lots and coastal exposure create different cleaning needs

Nassau County properties range from newer subdivisions to wooded and coastal-area homes. Shade, tree cover, roof debris, exterior organic buildup and weather exposure can affect houses, roofs, gutters, concrete and fences differently.

House washing should match the exterior material

Compatible stucco, vinyl siding, fiber cement, painted surfaces, soffits and trim can be evaluated for house washing. Lower-pressure cleaning is often more appropriate than using flatwork pressure on building finishes.
