Pool Cage vs. Screen Enclosure Cleaning: What’s Included and How They’re Cleaned

Compare pool-cage and screen-enclosure cleaning, including frames, screens, decks, organic buildup and pool-water protection.

Updated August 17, 2026 · Jacksonville Pressure Washing field guide

Written and reviewed by Jacksonville Pressure Washing, a licensed and insured exterior-cleaning company serving Jacksonville and surrounding Northeast Florida communities.

Quick answer: Pool cage and screen enclosure are often used as overlapping Florida terms, but a quote should still define the surfaces included: aluminum frame, screen panels, doors, lower tracks, deck or patio, and nearby coping are separate cleaning areas.

The enclosure is a system of different materials

Powder-coated aluminum, screen mesh, fasteners, doors and track areas do not all need the same pressure. Screens can tear or stretch, while oxidized frames may show chalking that routine washing does not restore.

The deck may be a separate surface

Concrete, pavers, painted decks and natural stone can sit inside the same enclosure. A quote for the cage or screen structure should clarify whether the floor or pool deck is included and which method will be used on it.

Pool water and runoff matter

Cleaning solution and dirty rinse water can travel toward the pool. Spray direction, dilution, drainage and protection of nearby furnishings and landscaping should be part of the job plan.

Oxidation is not ordinary dirt

White or chalky residue on aluminum can be coating oxidation rather than organic buildup. A standard enclosure wash may clean dirt and growth without restoring an oxidized finish, so those expectations should be separated before work begins.

What to inspect before pool-area cleaning

  • Frame and screen condition: Oxidized aluminum, loose spline and damaged mesh need conservative handling.
  • Deck material: Concrete, pavers, coatings and natural stone can require different methods.
  • Pool water: Spray and runoff should be managed so cleaning solution is not carelessly directed into the pool.
  • Furniture and landscaping: Outdoor living areas often have more sensitive items than an open driveway.

Why the quote should name each surface

“Pool cage cleaning” can mean only the frame and screens to one company and frame, screens and deck to another. Listing the enclosure, doors, tracks, deck, coping and adjacent surfaces separately makes the scope easier to compare and prevents assumptions about what is included.

The safest cleaning decision starts with the material, existing damage, coating condition, access and the type of staining present.

Frequently asked questions

Is a pool cage the same as a screen enclosure?

In Florida the terms are often used for similar screened structures, but the quoted scope should identify exactly which frames, screens and floor surfaces are included.

Can screen mesh be pressure washed?

Screens require controlled technique because excessive pressure can stretch, tear or detach mesh.

Does pool cage cleaning include the deck?

Only if the quote includes it. The enclosure frame, screen panels and pool deck can be separate service areas.
