House Washing Companies Near Me: What to Compare Before Hiring

A nearby contractor still needs the right process for stucco, siding, soffits, fascia, painted surfaces and Florida organic growth.

Updated August 11, 2026 · Jacksonville Pressure Washing field guide

Written and reviewed by Jacksonville Pressure Washing, a licensed and insured exterior-cleaning company serving Jacksonville and surrounding Northeast Florida communities.

Quick answer: When searching for a house washing company near you, compare more than distance and star ratings. Look for a company that identifies your exterior material, explains whether it will use soft washing or controlled pressure, understands oxidation and loose paint, protects landscaping and openings, carries current insurance, shows recent local work and defines exactly what the house-wash quote includes.

What separates house washing from general pressure washing?

A driveway-cleaning company can own excellent equipment and still not be the right choice for a delicate house exterior. House washing requires attention to material, paint condition, windows, vents, doors, electrical fixtures, landscaping and areas where water can be forced behind finishes.

In Jacksonville, homes commonly combine stucco, painted masonry, fiber-cement siding, vinyl, soffits, fascia, screened porches and exterior gutter faces. The method should be chosen around those surfaces.

Seven filters for choosing a nearby house washing company

  1. Service area: Confirm the company regularly works at your address.
  2. Surface knowledge: Ask how your exact siding or stucco will be cleaned.
  3. Low-pressure capability: Make sure the company does not rely on aggressive pressure for every wall.
  4. Oxidation awareness: Chalky paint or siding can react differently to washing and should be identified before work.
  5. Written scope: Confirm walls, soffits, fascia, entry areas and exterior gutter faces.
  6. Property protection: Ask about plants, outlets, cameras, door hardware, windows and furniture.
  7. Local proof: Look for recent work on houses similar to yours.

Soft washing is often the better house-washing method

Soft washing relies more on appropriate cleaning solution and lower-pressure application/rinsing than on blasting the surface. That approach is commonly used for stucco, siding and painted exteriors where high pressure can cause damage or force water into openings.

This does not mean every company using the words “soft wash” follows the same process. Ask what pressure reaches the surface, how solution is mixed and how landscaping is protected.

Watch for oxidation and paint condition

Sun and weather can leave chalky oxidation on painted aluminum, vinyl or other finished surfaces. Washing may reveal uneven oxidation or disturb the chalky layer. Loose or failing paint can also lift during cleaning.

A professional should identify obvious pre-existing conditions and explain what cleaning can and cannot fix.

House washing red flags

  • “We use maximum pressure to get it cleaner.”
  • No questions about siding, stucco or paint condition.
  • Every stain is promised to disappear.
  • No written explanation of what the house wash includes.
  • No plan for plants, outlets, cameras or door seals.
  • Roof cleaning is offered with the same high-pressure method as the driveway.
  • Exterior gutter brightening is promised as part of a basic rinse without discussing tiger striping or oxidation.

Photo-quote tip: Send all four sides of the house, the front entry, any screened porch and close-ups of heavy growth or stains. Tell the contractor whether you want the driveway, roof or gutters quoted at the same time.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find a reputable house washing company near me?

Use local search to find companies that serve your address, then compare insurance, surface-specific methods, detailed reviews, written scope and property protection.

Should my Jacksonville stucco house be pressure washed?

Stucco commonly benefits from lower-pressure cleaning rather than aggressive pressure. The correct method depends on paint condition, cracks, stains and the specific stucco finish.

Does house washing include gutters?

It may include normal rinsing of exterior gutter faces, but interior debris removal and gutter brightening are separate services unless the quote says otherwise.

Does house washing include windows?

Windows are typically rinsed during exterior washing, but detailed glass cleaning or spot-free window service should be treated as a separate scope unless included in writing.
