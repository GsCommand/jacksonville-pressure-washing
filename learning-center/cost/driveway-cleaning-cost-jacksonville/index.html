How Much Does Driveway Cleaning Cost in Jacksonville?

Square footage sets the scale, but pretreatment, sidewalks, curbs, oil, rust and access determine whether two driveways of similar size cost the same.

Updated August 11, 2026 · Jacksonville Pressure Washing field guide

Written for Jacksonville homeowners comparing concrete and driveway-cleaning quotes.

Quick answer: Published Jacksonville pricing references commonly place an approximately 800-square-foot driveway around $150–$260, about 1,200 square feet around $230–$400, and larger 1,600–2,000-square-foot driveways higher. Those are market references, not a quote. Minimum charges, heavy staining, oil treatment, rust removal, sidewalks and difficult drainage can move the final price.

What changes a driveway quote?

  • Square footage: Larger drives require more time, water, pretreatment and rinsing.
  • Minimum service charge: Very small driveways may still have a minimum because setup and travel do not disappear.
  • Organic buildup: Heavy algae or mildew may require more pretreatment.
  • Oil: Degreasing and agitation can be separate from standard cleaning.
  • Rust / irrigation staining: Usually requires specialty chemistry.
  • Sidewalks and curbs: Added flatwork increases measurable area and edge work.
  • Drainage: Poor runoff or sensitive surrounding areas can slow rinsing.

Per-square-foot pricing vs. flat pricing

Square-foot pricing is useful for larger, open concrete because area correlates with labor. Smaller jobs are often quoted as a flat amount because a minimum charge matters more than the exact square footage.

For a meaningful comparison, ask whether the quoted area includes the sidewalk apron, city sidewalk, front walk, curb and patio.

Does pretreatment cost extra?

Some companies include standard organic pretreatment in the base driveway-cleaning scope. Others price it separately. Specialty stain chemistry for oil, rust or irrigation iron is more likely to be an add-on.

Can a cheaper quote be just as good?

Yes—if the scope matches. Compare whether both quotes include pretreatment, a surface cleaner, edges, rinsing and the same stain expectations. A lower price for a smaller scope is not the same offer.

Should you bundle the house or sidewalks?

Bundling can make sense because setup and travel overlap. Ask for separate line items for the driveway, house, sidewalks and other surfaces so you can see whether the package actually saves money.

Frequently asked questions

What is the average driveway-cleaning cost in Jacksonville?

Public local guides put an 800-square-foot driveway around $150–$260, with larger drives scaling upward. Site conditions can shift the quote.

Does oil stain removal come with driveway washing?

Not always. Old oil can require separate chemistry and may not disappear completely even after treatment.

Does a circular driveway cost more?

Usually if it has more square footage or edge work. Shape matters less than total area, access and complexity.

How do I get the most accurate quote?

Send the property address, wide driveway photos and close-ups of oil, rust or other specialty stains.
