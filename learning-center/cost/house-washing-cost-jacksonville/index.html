Jacksonville House Washing Cost Guide

How Much Does House Washing Cost in Jacksonville, FL?

Use published local pricing as a planning range, then compare the actual wall area, stories, material, access and scope at your property.

Updated August 11, 2026 · Jacksonville Pressure Washing field guide

Pricing examples below are planning ranges from publicly posted Jacksonville-area pricing and 2026 local cost guides. They are not a universal rate sheet.

Quick answer: Public Jacksonville pricing examples show house washing starting around $179–$199, while broader 2026 local guides place many standard house exterior washes around $184–$460. Larger two-story homes, difficult access, extensive porches, heavy buildup, specialty stains, screens or bundled services can push the final price higher.

Why home square footage is not the whole price

A 2,000-square-foot single-story ranch and a 2,000-square-foot two-story home can have very different exterior wall area and access. Conditioned square footage is useful context, but it does not directly measure the amount of siding, stucco, fascia, porch ceiling or trim being cleaned.

For photo estimates, front/rear/side views and the property address are more useful than indoor square footage alone.

What changes house-washing cost?

One story vs. two stories

Second-story walls, tall gables and difficult rooflines can increase setup and cleaning time. Low-pressure application can reach many areas from the ground, but access still affects production.

Stucco vs. siding

Different materials require different handling. Stucco cracks, chalky oxidation, painted surfaces and siding seams can change how solution is applied and rinsed.

Amount of organic growth

Light pollen and routine algae are different from heavy shaded growth that requires more treatment and dwell time.

Porches, columns and architectural detail

Large covered patios, columns, decorative trim, balconies and complex entries add surface area that may not be obvious from the home’s square-foot number.

Exterior gutter faces

A normal house wash may rinse organic buildup from gutters, but “tiger stripes” or bonded oxidation can require separate gutter brightening.

Specialty stains

Rust, irrigation iron, artillery fungus, oxidation and paint failure are not standard organic house washing. Ask whether specialty treatment is included or quoted separately.

What should be included in the price?

  • Defined exterior wall surfaces.
  • The cleaning method and normal detergent treatment.
  • Reasonable protection of landscaping and nearby items.
  • Rinsing of cleaned surfaces.
  • Clear explanation of soffit, fascia, porch and gutter-face scope.
  • Known exclusions for specialty stains, oxidation or damaged paint.
  • Cleanup and a final check.

Quote comparison tip: A $199 house wash and a $349 house wash are not automatically the same service. One may cover only exterior walls while another includes soffits, fascia, entry areas and more detailed rinsing. Compare the scope before the number.

Can bundling lower the effective price?

Often, yes. A house wash, driveway and gutters completed during one visit share travel, setup and hose deployment. Some local companies publicly advertise bundled exterior packages. The savings only matter if the bundle includes the services you actually need.

Frequently asked questions

How much should it cost to wash a 2,000-square-foot house in Jacksonville?

There is no reliable price from indoor square footage alone. Public Jacksonville guides put many standard house washes roughly in the $184–$460 range, but stories, wall area, material, access and scope can move the quote.

Does two-story house washing cost more?

Often yes because of added exterior wall area, height, access and setup.

Is driveway cleaning included in house washing?

Usually not unless the quote specifically bundles it. House washing and concrete cleaning use different processes.

Is gutter cleaning included?

Interior gutter debris removal is normally separate. Exterior gutter faces may receive a normal rinse with a house wash, while gutter brightening is a separate specialty service.
