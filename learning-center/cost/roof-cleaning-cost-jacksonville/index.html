How Much Does Roof Cleaning Cost in Jacksonville?

Roof size matters, but pitch, material, story count, access, organic growth and landscaping protection often explain why two similar-looking homes receive different quotes.

Updated August 11, 2026 · Jacksonville Pressure Washing field guide

Written and reviewed by Jacksonville Pressure Washing, a licensed and insured exterior-cleaning company serving Jacksonville and surrounding Northeast Florida communities.

Quick answer: Published Jacksonville 2026 pricing guides commonly place professional roof cleaning in the several-hundred-dollar range, with some local estimates around roughly $230–$550 for simpler projects and broader local soft-wash guides showing about $0.25–$0.75 per roof square foot, or roughly $375–$1,200 for many 1,500–2,000-square-foot roofs. Those ranges are market references, not a quote. Roof type, pitch, access and treatment conditions can move the price substantially.

Jacksonville context: Roof-cleaning cost in Jacksonville is shaped by roof material, pitch, height, access, landscaping, runoff management and roof condition. Northeast Florida homes may have asphalt shingles, concrete tile, clay tile or metal roofing, and those systems should not be treated as one generic cleaning job. View Jacksonville service coverage.

Roof area is only the starting point

Square-foot pricing is useful because it gives both sides a way to talk about scale. But a 2,000-square-foot simple ranch roof is not the same job as a 2,000-square-foot steep roof with multiple valleys, dormers, a pool enclosure below and dense landscaping around the perimeter.

Ask whether the company is pricing the home's living area, roof footprint or actual roof surface area. Those are not the same measurement.

What should be included in a roof-cleaning quote?

  • Roof sections and detached structures being treated
  • Cleaning method appropriate to the roof material
  • Access assumptions and whether roof walking is required
  • Landscaping and runoff protection
  • Handling of gutters and downspouts
  • Expected treatment of black streaks and organic growth
  • Clear exclusions for permanent staining, rust, coating failure or roof damage
  • Scheduling and weather expectations

Example pricing framework

Use market numbers to screen a quote, not to force every property into a fixed price. A very low quote may be legitimate for a small, accessible roof, while a four-figure quote may be reasonable for a larger tile roof or a complicated two-story property.

Better comparison: Ask three companies to quote the same roof sections and explain the same protection steps. Then compare price. Otherwise you may be comparing three different jobs.

Does roof material affect price?

Yes. Material can change both risk and labor. Asphalt shingles, tile and metal may require different access decisions, spray approach, dwell time and rinse strategy. Tile can also introduce breakage concerns when access requires foot traffic.

Does heavy black streaking cost more?

It can. Light organic staining and a heavily colonized roof are not necessarily the same treatment. More growth may require additional dwell time, solution, application passes or return expectations. The contractor should explain whether one application is expected to produce an immediate cosmetic result or whether weathering continues the process afterward.

Roof cleaning vs. full exterior package

Bundling a roof with a house wash, gutter cleaning or driveway cleaning can change the total because setup, travel and property protection overlap. Ask for line-item pricing if you want to understand the value of the package.

Roof condition and access can matter more than square footage

A low-slope single-story shingle roof with clear access is a different project from a steep tile roof above a screened pool enclosure. Quotes should define the cleaning method, whether detached structures are included, how runoff and plants are protected and what pre-existing roof damage may limit the work.

Common questions

What is a normal roof-cleaning price in Jacksonville?

Several-hundred-dollar quotes are common in published local guides, but roof complexity can push a project lower or much higher. Use the range as context, not as a guaranteed price.

Is $200 too cheap for roof cleaning?

Not automatically. A small, simple project can price lower than a large roof. The better question is what roof area, method and property protection are included.

Is roof cleaning worth bundling with house washing?

Often it is worth asking for both prices because setup overlaps, but the bundle only makes sense if you actually need both services.

Should I compare price per square foot?

It is useful as one benchmark, but pitch, height, material, access and protection needs can matter as much as area.
