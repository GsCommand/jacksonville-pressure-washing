How Much Does Pressure Washing Cost in Jacksonville, FL?

A practical 2026 guide to driveway, house, roof and full-exterior pricing—and why two quotes for the same address can be very different.

Updated August 11, 2026 · Jacksonville Pressure Washing field guide

Written and reviewed by Jacksonville Pressure Washing, a licensed and insured exterior-cleaning company serving Jacksonville and surrounding Northeast Florida communities.

Quick answer: In Jacksonville, small single-surface pressure-washing jobs commonly begin in roughly the mid-$100s. Public 2026 local examples show driveway cleaning starting around $179, house washing starting around $179–$199, and broader guides putting standard house exterior cleaning roughly in the $184–$460 range. A local Jacksonville cost guide places many single-surface jobs around $150–$725 and full exterior packages around $800–$2,000+ depending on scope. Your actual quote should be based on the property, not an internet average.

What actually changes the price?

1. Surface area

Driveways and concrete are often estimated from square footage or by a standard size category. House washing can be influenced by home footprint, stories and exterior wall area. Roof work adds pitch and access.

2. One story vs. two story

Height affects setup, hose management, ladder or pole work, solution delivery and time. Two homes with the same conditioned square footage can have very different exterior-cleaning difficulty.

3. Surface type

Concrete can be mechanically cleaned with controlled pressure and surface-cleaning equipment. Stucco, siding, painted trim, screens and roofs often need lower-pressure methods and more attention to chemistry and rinsing.

4. Organic growth vs. specialty stains

Algae and mildew are different from oil, rust, irrigation iron, oxidation, paint transfer or battery stains. Specialty chemistry and repeat treatment may be separately priced.

5. Access and setup

Gates, long hose runs, steep slopes, roof height, screened enclosures, limited water access and crowded patios can add labor even when the actual surface is not especially large.

6. Bundled services

A house wash plus driveway, sidewalk and gutter cleaning may be priced more efficiently than four separate visits because travel and setup happen once. Always compare the bundled scope—not just the discount percentage.

Minimum service charges

Professional companies commonly have a minimum because the job includes travel, setup, chemical preparation, hose deployment, property protection, cleaning, rinsing, cleanup and payment processing even when the requested surface is small. One Jacksonville provider publicly lists a $125 starting minimum, while other local pressure-washing companies publish starting prices around $179.

How to get a more accurate quote from photos

  • Send the property address.
  • Take one wide photo of each requested surface.
  • Add close-ups of oil, rust, irrigation stains, oxidation or heavy organic growth.
  • Show difficult access, gates, steep grades or screened areas.
  • State whether you want one surface or a package.
  • Ask what is included and what is excluded.

A photo quote can often establish a useful range, but unusual access, hidden surfaces or specialty stains may still require confirmation.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to pressure wash a driveway in Jacksonville?

Public Jacksonville pricing examples and local 2026 guides commonly put standard residential driveway cleaning in the low-to-mid hundreds, with examples from about $125 to $375 depending on size and condition.

How much does house washing cost in Jacksonville?

Public local starting prices can be around $179–$199, while broader 2026 Jacksonville guides place many house exterior washes roughly around $184–$460. Larger, taller or more complex homes can cost more.

Why is roof cleaning more expensive than a driveway?

Roof work adds height, pitch, material-specific cleaning, solution control, landscaping protection and more safety/setup considerations.

Can I get one price for the whole property?

Yes. Ask for a written package showing exactly which surfaces are included so you can compare it with individual service quotes.
