Jacksonville Cost Guide

How Much Does Gutter Cleaning Cost in Jacksonville?

The price is usually driven by height, gutter length, debris, guards and access—not just the square footage of the house.

Updated August 11, 2026 · Jacksonville Pressure Washing field guide

Written and reviewed by Jacksonville Pressure Washing, a licensed and insured exterior-cleaning company serving Jacksonville and surrounding Northeast Florida communities.

Quick answer: For Jacksonville Pressure Washing, simpler residential gutter-cleaning jobs can start around $149–$199, while two-story homes, larger gutter systems, heavy debris, gutter guards and difficult access can cost more. Broader consumer pricing guides commonly place residential gutter cleaning around $100–$250 nationally, with multi-story homes extending higher. A photo quote is more useful than a generic average.

One-story vs. two-story gutter cleaning

Height changes more than ladder length. Setup takes longer, ladder positioning can be restricted by landscaping or roof design, and rear elevations over pool cages or patios may require a different access plan.

That is why a two-story home with the same gutter footage as a one-story home can still cost more.

What should the quoted price include?

  • Interior leaf and debris removal
  • Handling of accessible downspouts
  • Basic cleanup and debris handling
  • Access assumptions
  • Whether before-and-after photos are included
  • Any gutter-guard work
  • Clear exclusions for repair, re-pitching or damaged gutter components

Gutter cleaning and gutter brightening are different

Interior cleaning restores the drainage channel. Gutter brightening treats the outside gutter face for staining or tiger striping. If appearance is your concern, ask for both scopes separately.

Do not compare unlike quotes: One company may include exterior face cleaning and another may quote only debris removal. Make the scopes match before comparing price.

How photo quoting helps

Send clear photos of all four sides of the house, plus close-ups of guards, pool enclosures and heavy tree coverage. That lets the contractor estimate stories, access and visible gutter complexity before arrival.

Is recurring gutter cleaning cheaper?

Routine service can be easier to price because the company learns the property and debris is less likely to become heavily compacted. A recurring plan also reduces the chance that a neglected gutter becomes an emergency overflow problem during a storm.

Frequently asked questions

What does gutter cleaning start at in Jacksonville?

Our current starting framework is around $149–$199 for simpler residential jobs, with larger and more difficult properties priced from photos or inspection.

Do gutter guards increase the price?

They can if guards must be removed, lifted or cleaned separately to access debris underneath.

Are downspouts included?

The quote should say whether accessible downspouts are checked or flushed. Major blockages or disassembly can be separate work.

Does gutter cleaning include repairs?

Not automatically. Loose fasteners, bad pitch, leaks and damaged sections are repair issues unless the quote specifically includes them.
