Metal Roof Cleaning in Florida: Soft Washing vs. Pressure Washing

What to consider before cleaning a painted or coated metal roof in Florida, including coatings, seams, oxidation and runoff.

Updated August 17, 2026 · Jacksonville Pressure Washing field guide

Written and reviewed by Jacksonville Pressure Washing, a licensed and insured exterior-cleaning company serving Jacksonville and surrounding Northeast Florida communities.

Quick answer: Metal roofs are durable, but the visible finish, seams, fasteners, oxidation and manufacturer instructions still control the cleaning method. Do not assume that a strong substrate means aggressive pressure is appropriate for the coating.

Metal roof does not mean one surface type

Standing-seam panels, exposed-fastener systems and different factory coatings can all be called metal roofs. The substrate may be strong while the finish is more sensitive. Identifying the roof system and coating is the first step.

Organic buildup and oxidation are different

Algae, pollen and surface grime may be removable with routine cleaning. Faded, chalky or oxidized coating is a finish condition. Washing can reveal oxidation more clearly but should not be represented as coating restoration.

Seams and runoff need attention

Roof cleaning introduces water and treatment solution around seams, penetrations, gutters and landscaping below. Spray direction and runoff management matter even when the panel itself is durable.

Manufacturer instructions should lead

Metal-roof manufacturers can specify compatible cleaners, concentrations and methods for their coatings. When those instructions are available, they should take priority over a generic pressure-washing recipe.

What to inspect before roof cleaning

  • Roof material: Asphalt shingles, clay tile, concrete tile and coated metal each have different cleaning limits.
  • Age and condition: Broken tile, loose shingles, failed coatings and flashing issues are roofing conditions, not cleaning problems.
  • Pitch and access: Roof work adds fall and slip hazards that should influence the service plan.
  • Runoff path: Gutters, landscaping, siding, pools and hardscape below the roof can receive cleaning runoff.

When roof cleaning is not enough

Cleaning can address compatible organic staining, but it does not fix leaks, deteriorated roofing, failed flashing or structural problems. If the roof shows damage beyond surface discoloration, a roofing inspection may be more important than cleaning. Manufacturer cleaning guidance should also take priority over a generic exterior-washing method.

For a property-specific cleaning decision, inspect the material, existing damage, access, runoff and specialty staining before choosing pressure or chemistry.

Frequently asked questions

Can metal roofs be pressure washed?

Some systems may tolerate controlled cleaning, but coating condition and manufacturer guidance should determine the method.

Will washing remove oxidation from a metal roof?

Routine cleaning is not the same as restoring an oxidized or faded coating.

Is soft washing used on metal roofs?

Low-pressure treatment can be appropriate for some roof systems, but chemistry and coating compatibility must be checked.
