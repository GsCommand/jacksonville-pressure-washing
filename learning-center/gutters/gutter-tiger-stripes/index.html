What Are Gutter Tiger Stripes? How Gutter Brightening Removes Black Streaks

Why dark vertical streaks form on gutter faces and why exterior gutter brightening is different from cleaning debris out of the gutter channel.

Updated August 17, 2026 · Jacksonville Pressure Washing field guide

Written and reviewed by Jacksonville Pressure Washing, a licensed and insured exterior-cleaning company serving Jacksonville and surrounding Northeast Florida communities.

Quick answer: Gutter tiger stripes are dark vertical streaks on the outside face of gutters. They are a different problem from leaves and debris inside the gutter, so an interior gutter cleanout does not automatically brighten the exterior face.

Jacksonville context: Jacksonville and Northeast Florida gutters deal with frequent rain, humidity, roof runoff and airborne organic debris. That makes it especially important to separate a drainage problem from an exterior appearance problem. Our Jacksonville service area covers homes where both issues may be present at the same time.

Interior cleaning and exterior appearance are separate scopes

Gutter cleaning usually focuses on removing leaves, roof grit and debris from the channel and checking accessible downspouts. Tiger stripes are on the exterior painted face. A gutter can drain correctly and still look streaked from the ground.

Why stronger water pressure is not the whole answer

The streaking can bond to the painted gutter surface. More pressure can increase the risk of marking paint without addressing the chemistry of the stain. Exterior brightening is therefore treated as a separate cleaning process.

Paint condition still matters

Older gutters can have oxidation, chalking, faded paint or previous abrasion. Those conditions can limit how uniform the final appearance becomes. A professional should distinguish removable streaking from coating deterioration before promising a result.

How this fits with a house wash

A standard house wash can remove general organic film from gutters and fascia, but bonded exterior gutter streaks may remain. If appearance of the gutter face matters, ask whether gutter brightening is included or quoted separately.

What to inspect around a gutter system

  • Channel debris: Leaves, roof grit and compacted organic material can restrict flow.
  • Outlets and downspouts: A clean-looking gutter can still have a blockage below the visible channel.
  • Pitch and attachment: Sagging, separated or incorrectly pitched gutters may hold water even after cleaning.
  • Exterior coating: Tiger stripes, oxidation and faded paint are appearance issues separate from drainage.

Cleaning versus gutter repair

Gutter cleaning can restore flow when debris is the cause, but it cannot correct failed seams, loose hangers, poor pitch, undersized drainage or damaged downspouts. Exterior brightening is also a separate cosmetic scope. Identifying the actual problem before service prevents a cleaning job from being expected to solve a repair or coating issue.

Why tiger stripes need a different evaluation from gutter debris

Tiger stripes are an exterior-face condition, so the first question is whether the gutter coating is still sound. A painted aluminum gutter with bonded surface streaking is a different job from a faded or oxidized gutter where the coating itself has changed. Cleaning may improve removable contamination, but it should not be presented as paint restoration.

The practical homeowner test is simple: look at drainage and appearance as two separate scopes. Leaves, roof grit and compacted debris affect flow inside the channel. Dark vertical streaks affect the visible face. One service can be completed correctly while the other issue remains.

What should be checked before gutter brightening

  • Paint condition: Look for chalking, fading, peeling or previous abrasion before stronger exterior chemistry is used.
  • Streak pattern: Confirm the marks are on the exterior face rather than overflow staining coming from a drainage problem.
  • Nearby finishes: Painted fascia, siding, windows and landscaping below the gutter can affect how runoff is managed.
  • Expectations: Ask whether the quoted service is a basic exterior wash or a dedicated gutter-brightening treatment.

Questions worth asking before service

  • Is interior debris removal included, exterior brightening included, or both?
  • Will oxidation or coating wear limit the final appearance?
  • Are downspouts being checked for drainage separately from the cosmetic work?

Common questions

Are tiger stripes inside the gutter?

No. The term usually refers to vertical staining on the outside face of the gutter.

Will normal gutter cleaning remove tiger stripes?

Not necessarily. Interior debris removal and exterior brightening are different scopes.

Can gutter paint oxidation affect the result?

Yes. Oxidation and coating wear can limit how evenly the gutter face responds to cleaning.
