Surface-appropriate cleaning

Use pressure where it belongs. Soft wash where it does not.

Concrete and other durable flatwork can benefit from controlled pressure and surface-cleaner equipment. Stucco, siding, roofs, soffits and painted materials often require chemical treatment and much lower rinse pressure.

Common Florida exterior problems

  • Black roof streaks and algae growth
  • Green organic buildup on siding and stucco
  • Mildew around shaded soffits and pool cages
  • Dark driveways, sidewalks and curbs
  • Orange irrigation and rust staining
  • Gutter debris, exterior grime and tiger striping
