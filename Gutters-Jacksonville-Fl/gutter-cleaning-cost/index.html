What affects the price

Gutter cleaning is priced around access, height and actual scope

A small one-story home with open access and light debris is very different from a large two-story home with steep rooflines, packed gutters or gutter guards.

We can often narrow down pricing from the property address plus clear exterior photos. For simple homes, that means you may not need an onsite estimate before scheduling.

Common pricing factors

  • One-story vs. two-story rooflines
  • Total gutter length and home footprint
  • Heavy leaves, pine needles or compacted debris
  • Gutter guards or screens
  • Difficult ladder access
  • Blocked or slow downspouts
  • Detached garages or additional structures

What a standard gutter cleaning includes

The goal is to clear the drainage system cleanly and verify that water has a path away from the roofline.

  • Loose gutter debris removed
  • Debris collected rather than intentionally blown across the yard
  • Accessible downspouts checked and flushed as needed
  • Before-and-after photos available
  • Problem areas identified for the homeowner

Services that may cost extra

  • Gutter guard removal or reinstallation
  • Severely packed or rooted debris
  • Difficult multi-level access
  • Detached structures
  • Exterior gutter brightening
  • Specialty repairs or damaged gutters

Gutter Cleaning Cost FAQ

How much does gutter cleaning cost in Jacksonville?

Simple residential jobs commonly start around $149–$199. Larger, taller or more difficult properties are quoted based on actual scope.

Does two-story gutter cleaning cost more?

Usually, yes. Additional height, roof configuration and ladder access can increase time and safety requirements.

Are downspouts included?

Accessible downspouts can be checked and flushed as part of the quoted scope. Severe underground or buried drain blockages are a separate issue.

Is gutter brightening included?

No. Interior gutter cleaning removes debris. Gutter brightening is a separate exterior cleaning process for visible gutter faces and tiger striping.

Can I get a gutter-cleaning estimate from photos?

Often, yes. Send the property address plus clear photos showing the front, sides, roof height and gutter access.
