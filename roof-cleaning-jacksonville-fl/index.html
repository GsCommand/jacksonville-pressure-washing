Roof cleaning Jacksonville FL

Black roof streaks are a cleaning problem—but the roof material controls the solution

Jacksonville homeowners search for roof cleaning, roof washing, roof cleaning near me, soft wash roof cleaning, roof algae removal and black streak removal. The important question is not which phrase was used. It is what material is on the roof and what is causing the discoloration.

What causes dark streaks on asphalt roofs?

The Asphalt Roofing Manufacturers Association says brown-to-black roof discoloration is commonly associated with algae and identifies Gloeocapsa magma as a prevalent species. ARMA notes that algae discoloration is especially widespread in warm, humid regions—conditions that make the topic highly relevant in Northeast Florida.

That discoloration can be mistaken for dirt, fungus, soot, moss or debris. Correct identification matters because the cleaning method for asphalt shingles should not be copied from a driveway, concrete tile or metal roof.

Read ARMA’s Algae Discoloration of Roofs technical bulletin.

Roof pressure washing vs. roof soft washing: a roof is not generic pressure-washing flatwork. Roof material, age, condition, coating, slope, drainage and manufacturer instructions all matter before a cleaning method is chosen.
Roof types we evaluate

Asphalt shingles, concrete tile, clay tile and metal roofs are not cleaned the same way

One of the biggest weaknesses in generic roof-cleaning pages is treating every roofing system as interchangeable. Jacksonville has multiple common roof materials, and each has different surface, coating, access and maintenance considerations.

Asphalt Shingle Roof Cleaning

Asphalt shingles can show black algae streaking, localized moss or organic discoloration. ARMA advises gently dispersing an approved cleaning solution and warns against scrubbing because friction can loosen granules. The shingle manufacturer’s instructions should control the final method. Aggressive driveway-style pressure washing is not the right starting point for asphalt shingles.

Concrete & Clay Tile Roof Cleaning

Tile roofing is a different system. Concrete tile can collect surface discoloration and organic growth, while clay tile may have a matte, glazed or other finish. Tile Roofing Industry Alliance guidance says professional low-intensity pressure washing can be used on concrete tile if desired, but roof condition, broken tiles, finish and manufacturer guidance still matter. See TRIA maintenance guidance.

Metal Roof Cleaning

Standing-seam and exposed-fastener metal roofs can develop dirt, pollen, organic growth or coating discoloration. Before cleaning, panel coating, oxidation, fasteners, sealants and manufacturer requirements should be considered. Metal should not automatically receive the same chemistry or mechanical cleaning used on shingles or tile.

Other roof materials: if the roof is slate, cedar, specialty coated, solar-integrated or otherwise outside the common residential systems above, the material should be identified before any cleaning method is promised.
Soft wash roof cleaning

Why roof cleaning is treatment-driven rather than pressure-driven

Soft wash roof cleaning uses lower water pressure than hard-surface pressure washing and relies more heavily on a roof-appropriate treatment process. That matters because the objective is to address organic discoloration without mechanically scouring the roofing surface.

ARMA’s current guidance for asphalt-shingle algae discoloration says the cleaning solution should be gently dispersed and specifically cautions against scrubbing. It also says other cleaning chemicals or methods should not be used without shingle-manufacturer approval.

Roof condition and access

The visible stain is only one part of a roof-cleaning decision

Before roof washing, a professional scope should consider the roof itself and everything the cleaning process interacts with around the property.

Roof Age & Visible Damage

Loose shingles, cracked or broken tile, failing coatings, exposed fasteners and other visible damage should be identified before cleaning. Cleaning is not roof repair.

Pitch, Access & Drainage

Roof slope, safe access, valleys, gutters, downspouts and runoff paths affect how a roof-cleaning project can be approached.

Landscaping & Adjacent Surfaces

Plants, painted finishes, metal fixtures, pool areas and neighboring surfaces can be affected by overspray or runoff and should be considered in the cleaning plan.

Professional roof-cleaning process

A six-step roof-cleaning decision process

The exact field procedure depends on roof material and condition, but the planning sequence should stay consistent and conservative.

1Identify roof materialDetermine whether the roof is asphalt shingle, concrete tile, clay tile, metal or another system before choosing a cleaning method.
2Review conditionNote visible damage, coatings, loose materials, broken tile, oxidation, moss, lichen and other conditions that may change the scope.
3Plan access and runoffConsider roof pitch, gutters, downspouts, valleys, surrounding landscaping and adjacent surfaces.
4Select roof-appropriate treatmentUse the roof manufacturer and relevant trade guidance rather than applying one generic pressure-washing method to every roof.
5Apply conservativelyAvoid unnecessary abrasion or aggressive mechanical cleaning on roofing materials that can be damaged by it.
6Review remaining discolorationSeparate expected post-treatment appearance from permanent staining, coating damage, rust or conditions that need roof repair rather than cleaning.
Moss, lichen, algae and roof stains

Not every roof mark should be treated—or promised—the same way

Black algae streaks, moss, lichen, tree debris, rust, coating discoloration and permanent material aging are different conditions. The safest response is to identify the condition first instead of assuming stronger chemistry or more pressure is automatically better.

On asphalt shingles, scraping or aggressive scrubbing can damage granules. On tile, walking and broken-tile risk can affect access. On metal, coating condition and oxidation can change what cleaning can realistically accomplish.

No universal instant-result promise: a responsible roof-cleaning page should explain that roof material, stain type and condition affect how quickly discoloration changes and whether every mark is removable.
HOA and insurance roof-cleaning requests

Cleaning can address appearance—but it is not a roof inspection or certification

The keyword research includes homeowners searching for roof cleaning after an HOA notice, insurance request or upcoming inspection. Those are real high-intent situations, but the scope needs to be stated carefully.

HOA Cleaning Notice

If the notice addresses visible roof staining or organic growth, a cleaning quote can be built around that condition. The homeowner should retain the notice and follow any community-specific requirements.

Insurance Request

A cleaning service can address exterior appearance when requested, but it does not replace a licensed roof inspection, certification, repair or insurer decision.

Pre-Sale / Inspection Preparation

Roof washing may improve curb appeal before a sale or inspection, but it should not be represented as correcting structural defects or extending roof life by a guaranteed number of years.

Roof cleaning FAQ

Jacksonville roof-washing questions

What causes black streaks on roofs in Jacksonville?

Brown-to-black roof discoloration is commonly associated with algae. The Asphalt Roofing Manufacturers Association identifies Gloeocapsa magma as a prevalent algae associated with roof discoloration, especially in warm and humid regions.

Should asphalt shingles be pressure washed?

Aggressive pressure and scrubbing are not the recommended approach for asphalt-shingle algae discoloration. ARMA advises gently dispersing an approved cleaning solution and cautions that scrubbing can loosen shingle granules. Manufacturer instructions should control the cleaning method.

Can concrete or clay tile roofs be cleaned?

Tile roofs can be cleaned, but concrete tile, clay tile, coatings, age, broken tiles and roof access all matter. Tile Roofing Industry Alliance guidance notes that professional low-intensity pressure washing can be appropriate for concrete tile when desired, while manufacturer and roof-condition guidance should still be followed.

Can metal roofs be washed?

Metal roofing can develop dirt, organic growth and atmospheric buildup, but panel finish, oxidation, fasteners, sealants and manufacturer cleaning requirements should be reviewed before cleaning. A metal roof should not automatically be treated like shingles or concrete tile.

What is soft wash roof cleaning?

Soft wash roof cleaning is a treatment-focused roof-cleaning approach that uses low water pressure compared with hard-surface pressure washing. The appropriate chemistry and procedure depend on roof material, condition and manufacturer guidance.

Can roof cleaning help with an HOA or insurance cleaning request?

Roof cleaning can address exterior staining or organic growth when a homeowner has received a cleaning request, but cleaning is not a roof inspection, roof certification or guarantee that an HOA or insurer will accept the roof. Follow the specific requirements in the notice.

Will every roof stain disappear immediately?

Results depend on roof material, stain type, age and condition. Organic discoloration can respond differently from rust, damaged coatings, embedded staining, moss or lichen. Roof cleaning should not be sold as a guarantee that every mark disappears immediately.
