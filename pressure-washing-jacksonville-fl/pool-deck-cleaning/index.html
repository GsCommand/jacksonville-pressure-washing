Pool deck cleaning Jacksonville

Pool-area flatwork needs cleaning that respects the surface and the pool around it

Pool decks are exposed to moisture, shade, pollen, foot traffic and repeated splash-out. In Jacksonville’s warm, humid environment, that can leave concrete and other deck materials looking dark or dingy. The cleaning method should be selected for the actual deck material—not simply because the area happens to be beside a pool.

Controlled cleaning for pool-deck surfaces

Compatible concrete may be cleaned with controlled pressure and surface-cleaner equipment. Pavers, coatings and decorative surfaces can require a more conservative approach. Existing cracks, joint condition, paint, coatings and sealer should all be considered before cleaning begins.

Pool areas also add another layer of planning: runoff, overspray, doors, screens, landscaping, furniture and the pool itself. Those details are part of the cleaning scope, not an afterthought.

Pool deck pressure washing: high pressure is not the goal. The goal is an even clean using the least aggressive method that is appropriate for the material and buildup.
Lanai cleaning Jacksonville

Lanais combine floors, walls, screens and outdoor living surfaces

A lanai can include concrete, pavers, tile, painted areas, enclosure framing, screens and adjacent house surfaces. That means one cleaning method may not be appropriate for the entire space.

This page focuses on the floor and deck surfaces. If the project also includes the enclosure itself, use the dedicated Pool Cage Cleaning and Screen Enclosure Cleaning pages.

Keeping the services separated helps homeowners understand what is being cleaned and lets the method be matched to each material.

Pool deck surface types

Different pool decks need different cleaning decisions

Concrete Pool Decks

Compatible concrete can often use controlled pressure and consistent surface-cleaner passes to address general organic buildup and dirt.

Pavers & Hardscape

Paver cleaning should account for joint sand, existing sealer, surface condition and the risk of disturbing joints with excessive pressure.

Painted or Coated Decks

Coatings and decorative finishes may require lower pressure, testing and a more conservative approach to reduce the risk of surface damage.

Common pool-area buildup

Algae, pollen, dirt and stains do not all respond the same way

Shaded pool decks can hold moisture and develop dark organic growth. Screened areas collect pollen and fine debris. Concrete near irrigation may show orange or rusty discoloration. Furniture, grills and foot traffic can leave additional marks.

  • Organic growth: may respond to appropriate treatment plus controlled cleaning.
  • Rust or irrigation staining: usually requires specialty stain treatment rather than pressure alone.
  • Oil or grease: can penetrate porous materials and may not completely disappear.
  • Permanent discoloration: may remain even when loose dirt and organic buildup are removed.

Specialty stain expectations should be discussed separately rather than treating every mark as something pressure alone can erase.

Professional pool deck cleaning process

How pool-area cleaning is planned

Pool-deck work follows the same surface-first approach as our other exterior cleaning services, with extra attention to water, drainage, enclosure materials and nearby finishes.

1InspectIdentify the deck material, coatings, cracks, joints, stains, drainage and nearby pool features.
2ProtectReview doors, landscaping, furniture, screens, electrical fixtures, pool water and sensitive finishes.
3Select methodChoose pressure, treatment and equipment appropriate for the material and visible buildup.
4Treat buildupAddress compatible organic growth and general grime with surface-appropriate cleaning chemistry where needed.
5Clean evenlyUse consistent passes and edge work rather than concentrated pressure in isolated spots.
6Rinse & reviewComplete the rinse and identify specialty stains or permanent marks that need separate expectations.

Pool deck cleaning FAQ

Common questions from Jacksonville homeowners

What is included in pool deck cleaning?

Pool deck cleaning is planned around the deck material, visible buildup, nearby pool water, doors, screens, landscaping and surrounding finishes. Compatible surfaces are treated and cleaned with pressure adjusted to the material.

Can algae be removed from a pool deck?

Organic buildup on compatible pool-deck surfaces can often be treated and cleaned with appropriate chemistry, controlled pressure and thorough rinsing.

Is pool deck pressure washing safe around pool water?

Pool-area cleaning requires additional care around water, drainage and chemical runoff. The work should be planned to manage overspray and runoff around the pool environment.

Do you clean pool cages with the pool deck?

Pool cage cleaning is available as a related service and has its own dedicated page. The pool deck and enclosure can be reviewed together when both are part of the requested scope.

Do you clean screen enclosures?

Yes. Screen enclosure cleaning is a related service with a dedicated page because enclosure frames and screens require a different cleaning approach than durable pool-deck flatwork.

How do I get a quote for pool deck cleaning?

Use the quote page and provide the property address, pool-deck material, surfaces to be cleaned and photos when available so the scope can be reviewed.

Need your pool deck or lanai cleaned?

Send the property address, pool-deck material, surfaces you want cleaned and photos when available. If the pool cage or screens are included, add those to the scope too.
