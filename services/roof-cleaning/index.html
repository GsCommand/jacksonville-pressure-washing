Not a high-pressure job

Roof cleaning should treat growth, not blast shingles

Residential roof cleaning is generally approached with a soft-wash process rather than aggressive high pressure. The goal is to treat organic growth while protecting the roof surface, landscaping and surrounding materials.

Roof type, age, visible damage, pitch, drainage, gutters and landscaping are reviewed before cleaning. Staining that is not organic growth may respond differently.

Typical roof-cleaning process

  1. InspectReview roof material, condition, access and runoff areas.
  2. ProtectPlan for landscaping, fixtures and controlled application.
  3. TreatApply an appropriate roof-cleaning solution at low pressure.
  4. ReviewEvaluate remaining streaks and areas that may weather out gradually.

Roof Cleaning FAQ

Do you pressure wash roofs?

High pressure is not the default approach. Residential roof cleaning typically uses soft-wash treatment appropriate to the roof material and condition.

Will black streaks disappear immediately?

Many organic stains respond quickly, but exact results depend on roof condition and the type of discoloration present.

Can roof cleaning be combined with house washing?

Yes, depending on scheduling and property scope. Combining exterior services can be efficient when planned together.
