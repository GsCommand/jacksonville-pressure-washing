Rust & Irrigation Stain Removal in Jacksonville

Targeted treatment for orange iron and mineral staining caused by irrigation overspray, metal runoff and related exterior sources.

Pressure alone usually does not solve rust staining

Rust and irrigation stains are chemical discoloration, not ordinary dirt. Effective removal generally requires surface-compatible specialty treatment rather than simply increasing pressure.

Results depend on how long the stain has been present, the substrate, previous coatings and whether the source is still active.

Common stained surfaces

  • Concrete driveways
  • Sidewalks and curbs
  • Masonry and walls
  • Fence surfaces
  • Areas hit by irrigation overspray
