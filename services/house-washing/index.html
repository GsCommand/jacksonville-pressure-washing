Why soft washing matters

Clean the house without blasting the finish

House washing should rely on the correct treatment and controlled rinse pressure rather than trying to force buildup off stucco, paint, siding or vented soffits.

We inspect exterior finishes, oxidation, landscaping, fixtures and pre-existing damage before cleaning. Standard house washing targets organic buildup; irrigation rust, oxidation and permanent discoloration may need separate treatment.

House Washing FAQ

Is soft washing safe for stucco?

The method is selected for the surface condition. Stucco and painted finishes are commonly cleaned with low-pressure treatment rather than aggressive pressure.

Does house washing include soffits and fascia?

It can. The exact scope is confirmed in the quote, including outside gutter faces and entry areas.

Will it remove irrigation rust?

Not necessarily. Rust and mineral staining usually require specialty treatment.
