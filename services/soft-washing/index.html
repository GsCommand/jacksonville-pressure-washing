Different tool for different surfaces

Soft washing treats organic growth instead of trying to blast it away

The method uses surface-appropriate treatment plus low-pressure application and rinsing. That makes it especially useful for roof systems, painted finishes, stucco, siding and vented roofline surfaces.

Pressure washing still has an important role on concrete and durable flatwork. The point is not to choose one method for everything.

Common soft-wash applications

  • Roof cleaning
  • House washing
  • Stucco and painted siding
  • Soffits, fascia and trim
  • Screen enclosures and selected pool-area surfaces
