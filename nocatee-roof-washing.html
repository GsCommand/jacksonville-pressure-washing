Roof Cleaning Method

Why soft washing is used instead of high pressure

Nocatee roof washing should treat organic staining without relying on aggressive pressure. Florida humidity, shade, tree cover, and warm temperatures can support algae and mildew on asphalt shingles, tile, and other roof surfaces.

A controlled treatment is applied at low pressure so it can work on the buildup without using a pressure-washer stream to cut across shingles or force water beneath roof components. The exact method depends on roof material, age, staining, weather, pitch, access, and surrounding property conditions.

Nocatee Conditions

Humidity, shade, and tree cover affect staining

Homes near ponds, preserves, mature landscaping, and shaded streets may experience different roof conditions than open, full-sun properties. North-facing roof planes and shaded sections often dry more slowly.

Downspouts may discharge into planting beds, onto pavers, or near pool equipment, so runoff planning should be adapted to the property rather than treated as one-size-fits-all.

Roof washing improves organic buildup but does not repair leaks, loose shingles, cracked tiles, failing coatings, flashing problems, or structural damage.

Nocatee roof washing questions

Do you pressure wash roof shingles?

No. Soft-wash roof cleaning is designed to treat organic staining without relying on aggressive pressure.

What causes black streaks on a roof?

Black roof streaks are commonly associated with organic growth that thrives in humid conditions. Inspection helps distinguish treatable buildup from wear or permanent discoloration.

How is landscaping protected?

The application and runoff are planned around plants and exterior surfaces. The exact process depends on roof access, drainage, weather, and property layout.

How long does a roof stay clean?

Results vary with shade, tree cover, humidity, roof material, drainage, and surrounding conditions. Cleaning removes current buildup but cannot prevent all future growth.
